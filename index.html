About Me

Hello and welcome to my portfolio!

I’m Namisha, a Technical Project Manager with 4+ years of experience delivering cloud, DevOps, web and mobile-first projects for clients in India and the Gulf. At Firecaat Private Limited, I’m the single point of contact for new client engagements, from requirements and onboarding to delivery and handover.

I work between engineering, QA, clients, resellers and vendors to plan deployments, run staging-to-production releases and resolve issues quickly. I also deliver ZATCA e-invoicing compliance for Gulf clients and manage reseller relationships across India and the Gulf.

My hands-on base in AWS, Linux and MySQL helps me work closely with technical teams and explain progress clearly to clients. I track work in Jira and keep project documentation and SOPs in Confluence.
