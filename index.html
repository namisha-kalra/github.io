About Me

Hello and welcome to my portfolio!

I'm Namisha, a project manager who loves the technical side of delivery. For over four years I've been taking cloud, DevOps, web and mobile products from the first client call all the way to a smooth go-live, for businesses in India and the Gulf.

At Firecaat, I'm the person clients talk to first and the one who keeps developers, testers and support working in the same direction. Clear meeting notes, well-documented processes and no-surprise releases are what I care about.

I'm not just a coordinator. I'm comfortable working with AWS, Linux and MySQL, so I can talk to developers and clients in the same language.

Before tech, I studied Journalism at Delhi University. That's why I'm good at explaining complex things simply. I'm also certified in Cyber Security and AWS Compute Services.
